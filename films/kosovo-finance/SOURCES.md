# Sources: Kosovo's financial system in 2025

All figures are for end-December 2025 unless a year is given. "Computed" marks figures worked out here from the published ones.

## Documents

- **AR25**: Central Bank of the Republic of Kosovo (CBK/BQK), *Annual Report 2025*, Prishtina, June 2026. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CBK_AnnualReport2025.pdf
- **FSR22**: CBK, *Raporti i Stabilitetit Financiar, Numër 22* (Financial Stability Report 2025, in Albanian), August 2026. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/BQK_FSR-22.pdf
- **AR24**: CBK, *Annual Report 2024*, June 2025. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/CBK_AR_2024.pdf
- **AR23**: CBK, *Annual Report 2023*, June 2024. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CBK_AR_2023.pdf
- **AR22**: CBK, *Annual Report 2022*. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CBK_AR_2022.pdf
- **AR21**: CBK, *Annual Report 2021*. https://bqk-kos.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CBK_AR_2021.pdf

AR25 notes that some 2025 financial-sector data are unaudited and may be revised.
